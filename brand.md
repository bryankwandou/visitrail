# VisitRail brand

VisitRail is the evidence-first operating layer for home care agencies. Its visual identity combines clinical restraint with a warm, human sense of movement.

## Core palette
- Rail Deep: `#0D2B23`
- Evidence Green: `#226B51`
- Proof Mint: `#C9F37B`
- Care Paper: `#F5F7F2`
- Signal Amber: `#F2AD4B`

## Typography
- Display: Manrope
- Interface and body: DM Sans

## Voice
Direct, calm, and specific. State what happened, what remains unknown, and who owns the next decision. Avoid inflated AI language, fear-based compliance copy, and unsupported promises.

## Mark
The VisitRail mark uses two offset rails inside a rounded square. It suggests a route, a verified handoff, and forward motion without relying on medical crosses or generic heart symbols.
