# Product Audit — July 24, 2026

## Hard verdict

A generic “AI operating system for home care” would be weak. The market already has scheduling, EVV, billing, caregiver apps, and family portals. VisitRail only earns a reason to exist if it becomes the evidence and exception layer that prevents unverifiable work from silently becoming a billable claim.

## Strengthened wedge

**Category:** Evidence-first care operations.

**Primary buyer:** Small and midsize U.S. home care agencies with reimbursement exposure, coordinator overload, and inconsistent caregiver documentation.

**Core promise:** Every billable visit has a defensible evidence trail; every exception has an accountable human decision; every family update is grounded in a finalized record.

## Business model

- Base platform fee per agency location.
- Active-client tier for EVV, notes, and family access.
- Premium compliance export and multi-location reporting.
- Optional evidence anchoring priced as an audit feature, not a token product.

## Defensibility

- Accumulated exception-resolution patterns and evidence quality benchmarks.
- Workflow integration around the billability gate, not a detachable chatbot.
- Trust earned through transparent rules, immutable review history, and scoped family access.

## Kill criteria

Do not continue if agencies will not put VisitRail in the billing path, if coordinators still resolve exceptions outside the system, or if deployment requires replacing the scheduling system before value appears. The integration strategy should allow VisitRail to begin as an evidence overlay.

## MVP acceptance

1. Manual or incomplete evidence cannot become billable.
2. A flagged visit cannot resolve without privileged documented review.
3. Drafting never marks an unsupported task complete.
4. Matching ranks required skill/language/schedule fit above weak candidates.
5. Family access reveals only finalized records for linked clients.
